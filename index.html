02 — About

Finance student, game developer

I study finance at Kedge Business School in Marseille, which is an unusual path for a game developer. Thinking in budgets, risk and incentives turns out to be useful when you're shipping a game on your own.

I model in Blender, build scenes in Godot, and keep a foot in drawing and music. Every project starts from a look I want to see on screen, then I work backwards to make it run. I also play a lot of games, because I believe the best game makers are players first.
